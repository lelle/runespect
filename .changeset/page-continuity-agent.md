A page a panel has spoken to keeps its id in the tab's `sessionStorage` and says `bye` when it goes. The next page in the tab names it in its hello (`replaces`). A page answers a `ping` with its hello without following, says whether its tab is visible, and says hello again when the tab is shown or hidden. A tab no panel has spoken to writes nothing.
